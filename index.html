About

Scope of work

I sit at the point where a business requirement becomes a working system, and stay there until it's stable in production. That means gathering requirements alongside stakeholders, configuring the solution, running or supporting UAT, coordinating deployment, and handling the support tickets that come in afterward.

Most of my work has been on loan origination and loan management platforms — systems where accuracy, audit trails, and uptime aren't negotiable. I've learned to read a process end-to-end: what a loan officer needs on screen, what the credit team needs in a report, and what breaks quietly in production if configuration is off by one rule.

I'm the person a project team calls when something in the live system doesn't match what was signed off — and the one who can trace it back to the requirement, the config, or the release.
